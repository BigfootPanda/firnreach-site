Firnreach is for **1 to 8 players**: play solo, or in co-op. Online play goes through **Steam**: no separate account,
no port forwarding.

## Before you start

- **Steam must be running** when you start the game. Without it, only LAN and direct-IP games are available.
- **Everyone needs the same game version.** Steam keeps you up to date; a game on another version shows as
  *different version* and can't be joined.
- The **Steam overlay** should be enabled (Steam > Settings > In Game). You use it to send invites.

## Hosting a game

1. Open **Multiplayer** and pick who can join under **Host a game**:
   - **Friends:** your Steam friends can join you straight from their friends list.
   - **Invite only:** only players you invite can join.
   - **LAN:** players on your local network see the game; Steam isn't needed.
2. Set the **max players** and the **match settings**, then press **Host**.
3. Invite people with **Shift+Tab** (the Steam overlay) or **Invite friends** in the lobby.
4. When everyone is ready, press **Start**. A new map is generated for each match.

## Joining a game

- **From Steam:** accept an invite, or right-click a friend who is hosting in your Steam friends list and choose
  *Join game*.
- **From the game list:** open **Multiplayer**, look under **Join a game** and press **Join**.
- **LAN / direct IP:** under **Advanced**, enter the host's address. This only works for LAN-mode games; Steam games
  are always joined through Steam.

You can join a match that's already running.

## In the lobby and during a match

- The host can **kick** a player (lobby, or Pause > Players). A kicked player can't rejoin that game.
- Anyone can **mute** another player's chat for themselves.
- Teammates can **upgrade each other's towers** (whoever upgrades pays). Only the builder can sell a tower.
- **Team votes:** skipping the day and calling a Frost Surge are voted on. Press **Y** or **N**.
- Leaving a match counts as a loss for you.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| No Steam options in Multiplayer | Start Steam first, then the game. |
| *Different version* next to a game | One of you needs to update. In Steam, restart to apply updates. |
| Invite does nothing | Check the Steam overlay is enabled, then try **Invite friends** in the lobby. |
| Can't join by IP | Direct IP only reaches LAN-mode games. Ask the host to invite you through Steam instead. |
