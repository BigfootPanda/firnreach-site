Walk up to the counter and press **E** to shop for supplies for your next match. Prices are in **Rime Essence** and
**Heartstone** (see [Crystals and resources](/wiki/resources/)).

<div class="wrap-table">

| Item | What it does | Type | Price | Most you can own |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Repair kit - patch a building mid-wave** | Supply: the first time one of your buildings drops under 30% HP (or would fall), it snaps back to full. | Supply (used up) | 30 Rime Essence | 5 |
| **Power cell - a burst of stored power** | Supply: +250 max POWER for the match, starting full. | Supply (used up) | 30 Rime Essence | 5 |
| **Supply sled - a head start** | Supply: +15 of each crystal at the start of the match. | Supply (used up) | 25 Rime Essence | 5 |
| **Hand warmer - warmth in the cold** | Supply: +3 max HP for the match. | Supply (used up) | 20 Rime Essence | 5 |

</div>

Supplies are used up: one of each packed supply goes into your next match. Mark a supply **LEAVE HOME** to keep it for later.
