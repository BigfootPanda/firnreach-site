## How to spot its attack

A slow hammer-fist: the blow lands half a second after the arm comes up. When its shell bursts it roars - then it runs, but takes full damage.

> Older than Skarnholt: a stone heart in a century of permafrost shell, and something in there wants out. Break the shell, then be quick.

## Ability

Shatter Armor: After taking enough damage, its armor breaks, increasing its speed and removing its damage reduction.
