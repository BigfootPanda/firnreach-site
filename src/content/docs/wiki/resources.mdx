## Crystals

You mine four colors of crystal on the ice. Colored crystals always give their own color; **gray rocks** give a few
crystals of a random color.

<div class="wrap-table">

| Crystal | What it builds |
|---|---|
| **Red** | builds power (Generators, Batteries). |
| **Purple** | builds guns (Turret, Artillery, Sniper). |
| **Amber** | builds support (Healers, Shield Projectors, Siphons). |
| **Obsidian** | builds walls and pays for upgrades. |

</div>

Carrying more than your capacity slows you; twice your capacity stops you. Spend crystals on buildings to lighten
the load.

### Mining specialty

Pick a crystal color you mine 50% faster (the others 10% slower). Co-op teams split the colors. You choose it on the match setup screen; it is saved with your profile.

## Heartstone

Every boss drops one, and about 1 in 30 gray rocks. It is heavy, and it only counts once you **bank** it: carry it to
your Base and press **E**. Heartstone still in your pocket when the match ends is lost. Banked Heartstone
is spent in the [Hollow's shops](/wiki/hollow/).

## Rime Essence

The pale wisps enemies drop: walk over them before they fade. Every player also earns a wage each time a wave's day
ends. You keep all your Essence on a win and half of what you collected on a loss (the wage is always kept).
Rime Essence is spent in the [Hollow's shops](/wiki/hollow/). Your first win pays a bonus.

## Power

Most buildings run on **power**. Generators make it, Batteries store it and pass it on. You carry your own
**POWER** too: look at a tower and hold **F** to feed it, or press **V** to auto-feed
the nearest tower that needs it. Your POWER refills at the Base.

## Tips the game gives you

The first time each of these comes up in a match, the game shows the tip:

- A BOSS wave: one huge enemy that shrugs off tower fire. Aim your own shots at its glowing weak spots - they hit for double.
- Gray rocks give a few crystals of a random color. Colored crystals always give their own color.
- Your load is getting heavy. Past your carry limit you move at half speed: spend crystals on buildings, and bank Heartstone at your Base.
- A Shield Wall's shield only works while it has power: keep it in reach of a Generator.
- A tower ran out of power and stopped firing. Build a Generator in its reach, or feed it your own POWER (F).
- Heartstone! It is heavy, and it only counts once you bank it at your Base. Heartstone still in your pocket when the match ends is lost.
- This tower runs on POWER. Look at it and hold F to feed it yours (commander view: select it, FEED POWER). Your POWER refills at the Base.
- Stone Door: press T at it to open or close it (anyone can, even with a gun in hand). Open, it lets enemies through too; it shuts itself 6 s after you're clear.
- Your first of each tower is free this match (the Base and the Bank excepted): the build bar marks them FREE. Upgrades still cost.
- Roles: you build your role's towers (the others grey out, marked with the role that builds them); everyone builds the Base, Stone Wall and Bank. Jack of all trades builds the basics up to level 7. A Tank taunts with C.
- Bank crystals at the Bank for the team: press Z facing it (or E for its panel). Anyone can take their own back plus a fair share of the rest each night.
- Hold Middle Mouse to ping your team: release on Build here, Help me, Danger... Tap it to mark what you aim at. Commander view: Alt + Right Mouse.
- No walls yet. In a few nights the waves get big enough to reach your Base. Block the way in with Spike Walls so your towers can hold them.
