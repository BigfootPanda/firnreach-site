The host picks these on the match setup screen before a match (and can change them in the lobby).

## Difficulty

<div class="wrap-table">

| Preset | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Tutorial** | A guided first match on shallow ice. 120 s prep, Obsidian-only upgrades. |
| **Easy** | Shallow ice. 120 s prep, Obsidian-only upgrades. |
| **Medium** | Deeper ice. 90 s prep, standard upgrade colors. |
| **Hard** | Deep ice. 60 s prep, randomized upgrade colors. |
| **Insane** | Right over the Sleeper. 30 s prep, randomized upgrade colors. |

</div>

On a brand-new profile the **Tutorial** is preselected and marked RECOMMENDED: three gentle waves that teach the
basics step by step.

## Match length

<div class="wrap-table">

| Length | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Short** | A quick night. About 15 minutes: 12 waves that ramp up fast. |
| **Standard** | A full night. About 30 minutes: 20 waves. |
| **Long** | A long one. Pack boots. About 45 minutes: 28 waves with longer nights to build. |
| **Endless** | The Long Night: the cage comes when the Base falls. After wave 10 the waves grow fast; survive wave 20 on Normal or harder to hold the Hollow. Your best wave is recorded. |

</div>

## Advanced

- **Prep time:** 120 s, 90 s, 60 s, 30 s. The daylight you get to build before the first wave.
- **Upgrade cost:** which crystal pays for your in-match player upgrades at the Base.

<div class="wrap-table">

| Upgrade cost | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Obsidian Only** | All player upgrades will only cost Obsidian crystals. |
| **Standard** | Uses specific element per stat (Ex: Speed=Amber, Yield=Obsidian). |
| **Randomized** | The color required for an upgrade changes randomly every time. |

</div>

## World

<div class="wrap-table">

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| Safe zone | 12 x 12 cells, 16 x 16 cells, 20 x 20 cells |
| Rock coverage | 60%, 70%, 80%, 90% |
| Crystals | 3% of rocks, 5% of rocks, 8% of rocks |
| Map | Random every match, or a fixed map |
| Secrets on the ice | On: Relics and echo stones can turn up out on the ice. Off: A plain match: no relics or echo stones are placed. |

</div>

## The deep lift

*Something hums below the Sealed Gate. The deep lift is not safe to ride yet.* Mend its brake in the deep workshop behind the
Sealed Gate, and a match can ride it down instead of playing on the surface ice:

Darker, more rock and ice, tougher enemies with the nasty ones from wave 1, the Leviathan first. 1.5x Rime Essence, 2 Heartstones a boss. Medium, Hard and Insane.
