## How to spot its attack

An orange cone fills in front of it for 2 s: get your buildings out of it - the frost breath hits everything inside. An orange ring under a gun means that gun is about to freeze; carried guns never do.

> The drill woke it. It freezes guns with a look, breathes frost through walls and drinks the warmth from what it breaks. It won't breathe on the Base; it wants what the Base is singing.

## Ability

Siege: an icy beam freezes your nearest gun for 3 s, and a warned frost-breath cone hits every structure in it for 300 (never the Base). Life Siphon: heals for a fifth of the damage it deals to buildings. Carried guns are never frozen - pick one up.
