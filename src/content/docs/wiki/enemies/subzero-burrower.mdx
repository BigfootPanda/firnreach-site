## How to spot its attack

It dives into the snow (you can't hit it down there) and bursts back out. Its bite lands 0.4 s after it rears up. Fire when it surfaces.

> The Rime's own miner. It tunnels through frozen earth faster than you mine light. Shoot it when it comes up for air.

## Ability

Burrow: Periodically becomes untargetable by moving underground.
