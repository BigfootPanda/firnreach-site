## How to spot its attack

It rears up, then bites about a third of a second later. One mite barely hurts; a swarm at the wall does.

> Frost ticks: a handful of frost on six legs, drawn to any warmth. Each one is nothing. The night makes thousands, and they win by what leaks through.
