Your pick has **two tips** and a **handle**, all bought and upgraded at the [Pick Smith](/wiki/hollow/pick-smith/).
Each tip has its own tier, and its own **focus**: the resource it brings in more of.

## Tiers

The pick takes its name and look from its tips.

<div class="wrap-table">

| Tier | Name | Price per tip | Mining speed per tip |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Basic pick | Starting pick | - |
| 2 | White pick | 75 Rime Essence + 1 Heartstone | +3% |
| 3 | Verdant pick | 150 Rime Essence + 2 Heartstone | +6% |
| 4 | Dusk pick | 275 Rime Essence + 3 Heartstone | +9% |
| 5 | Prismatic pick | 450 Rime Essence + 6 Heartstone | +12% |
| 6 | Heartstone pick | 700 Rime Essence + 12 Heartstone | +16% |

</div>

Each tier: +3% mining speed (+4% at tier 6) and more of the resource this tip focuses on.

## Tip focus

Pick what each tip focuses on: **Obsidian crystal**, **Amber crystal**, **Purple crystal**, **Red crystal**, **Gray rock**, **Heartstone**.

## Handles

<div class="wrap-table">

| Handle | What it does | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard handle** | No bonus either way: a plain turned hardwood haft. | Free |
| **Light handle** | Mines every piece 15% faster. | 250 Rime Essence + 3 Heartstone |
| **Heavy handle** | Each hit bites off 25% more crystal (the node runs out sooner) and gray rock gives 25% more. | 250 Rime Essence + 3 Heartstone |

</div>

You can switch between the handles you own at any time at the Pick Smith.
