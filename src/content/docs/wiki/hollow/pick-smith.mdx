Walk up to the counter and press **E** to shop for pickaxe upgrades. Prices are in **Rime Essence** and
**Heartstone** (see [Crystals and resources](/wiki/resources/)).

<div class="wrap-table">

| Item | What it does | Type | Price | Most you can own |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| **Standard handle - the one it came with** | No bonus either way: a plain turned hardwood haft. | Pick handle | Free | 1 |
| **Light handle - quick swings** | Mines every piece 15% faster. | Pick handle | 500 Rime Essence + 5 Heartstone | 1 |
| **Heavy handle - hits harder** | Each hit bites off 25% more crystal (the node runs out sooner) and gray rock gives 25% more. | Pick handle | 500 Rime Essence + 5 Heartstone | 1 |

</div>

## Pick tips

- **Left tip - its own tier and focus**: Each tier: +3% mining speed (+4% at tier 6) and more of the resource this tip focuses on.
- **Right tip - its own tier and focus**: Each tier: +3% mining speed (+4% at tier 6) and more of the resource this tip focuses on.

Each tip is upgraded one tier at a time. Tier prices (per tip):

<div class="wrap-table">

| Tier | Pick | Price | Mining speed per tip |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 2 | White pick | 150 Rime Essence + 1 Heartstone | +3% |
| 3 | Verdant pick | 300 Rime Essence + 3 Heartstone | +6% |
| 4 | Dusk pick | 550 Rime Essence + 6 Heartstone | +9% |
| 5 | Prismatic pick | 900 Rime Essence + 12 Heartstone | +12% |
| 6 | Heartstone pick | 1400 Rime Essence + 24 Heartstone | +16% |

</div>

See [The pick](/wiki/picks/) for tip focuses.
