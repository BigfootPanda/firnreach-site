Achievements are kept per profile and most give a small **permanent bonus**. Check your progress at the
**Records** station in the Hollow; lifetime totals count across every match you play.

## Building & Defense

<div class="wrap-table">

| Achievement | How to unlock | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Master Architect** | Build a total of 500 buildings across all playthroughs. | All buildings cost 10% fewer resources to construct (rounded down). |
| **Peak Performance** | Upgrade any single building to its maximum level (Level 8). | Upgrading buildings costs 5% fewer resources. |
| **Fort Knox** | Have a single Battery store over 100,000 power. | All Batteries have their maximum power storage increased by 10%. |
| **Flawless Defense** | Survive an entire wave (after wave 5) without losing a single building. | All buildings start with +5% maximum health. |
| **Jack of All Trades** | Build every type of building AND upgrade each type to level 5 in a single match. | Permanently start with +55 of each resource. |
| **Generator who?** | generate 100,000 total power from your character across all playthroughs. | Start every match with an extra +500 personal power capacity. |
| **Maze Master** | Build a maze that forces a single enemy to walk at least 50 tiles further than its direct route to reach your base. | Wall maximum health is increased by 15%. |
| **Glass Cannon** | Survive to wave 10 without building any defensive Walls. | Offensive towers cost 10% less power to fire. |
| **The Great Wall** | Have 50 Wall structures active simultaneously in a single match. | Walls passively repair themselves for 1 HP every 3 seconds. |
| **Sentry Duty** | Upgrade a single tower to Level 5 before Wave 5 begins. | Level 1 towers start with +10% base damage. |
| **Overcharged** | Keep 10 of your towers at maximum power capacity for an entire wave (its whole day). | Towers have a 5% chance to not consume any power when they fire. |
| **Demolition Expert** | Sell 250 buildings across all playthroughs. | Selling a building now refunds an additional 5% of its total cost. |

</div>

## Combat & Kill

<div class="wrap-table">
