What the Almanac's **PLAYER** tab tells you, with the default keys filled in. Every key can be rebound in
Settings; see [Controls](/wiki/controls/) for the full list.

## Two Views

- Tab switches between FIRST PERSON (you walk, mine and feed towers yourself) and the RTS commander view (you order your character around and place buildings).

## First Person

- WASD move, mouse look, Space jump.
- Hold Left Mouse facing a rock or crystal to mine it.
- Hold F facing a tower to feed it your personal power - Healers, Batteries, Shield Projectors and Siphons run on it (look at one: its amber bar is its store). V: auto-feed the nearest tower that needs it.
- Press T at a Stone Door to open or close it (anyone can).
- Walk up to a tower, look at it and press E for its menu (upgrade, sell, take its gun). B opens build mode.

## RTS View

- WASD / screen edges pan, mouse wheel zooms.
- Right Mouse on the ground to walk there, on a rock or crystal to mine it.
- B opens the build bar: pick a tower, then Left Mouse on the grid to place (it stays armed; Shift-click queues). Esc puts it away; Backspace cancels your orders.
- Left Mouse a placed tower to see its stats, upgrade it or sell it. On a tower that runs on power, FEED POWER (or F) sends your character to feed it; on a Stone Door, OPEN / CLOSE (or T). Click the Base (or press Home) for the mining / move upgrades. Click a crystal to see how much is left.
- Esc (or P) opens the pause menu.

## Survival

- Mine Red / Purple / Amber / Obsidian crystals (gray rocks give +5 of a random color). Carrying more than your capacity slows you; twice your capacity stops you.
- Place a Base early - it's what the enemies attack, and its level caps your upgrades.
- Generators power nearby towers; batteries store and relay power.
- Prep is the short daylight. Then the dark comes once a match and stays; the quiet between waves is a night to rebuild. A wave is beaten once most of it is down: the next one comes soon after, or when the night runs out, and you can call it early. A boss walks every few waves (every 5th in a Standard match, and always on the last). Run past the match's time and it's OVERTIME: every wave comes faster and hits harder. Survive them all to win; if the base falls and everyone is down, you lose.

## Pings + Marks

- No voice chat needed: tap Middle Mouse to mark what you aim at (a crystal: Mine here, an enemy: Enemy, a tower: Carry this, the ground: Go here). Hold Middle Mouse for the ping wheel and release on Build here, Carry this gun, Help me, Danger, Go here or On my way. In the commander view hold Alt and right-click (Right Mouse). Marks show your name and color, the distance, and an arrow at the screen edge; they last 8 seconds (2 at a time each).

## First Builds + The Team Bank

- Your first of each tower is free every match (not the Base or the Bank): the build bar marks those FREE. Upgrades always cost, and a free tower sells for nothing.
- The Bank (UTILITY) is the team's shared store: deposit crystals there (Z facing it, or E for its panel; in the commander view select it) and they stop weighing you down. Each night you may take back your own deposits plus an equal share of what the others put in. Every deposit and withdrawal shows in the team feed.
