Between matches you're back in the **Hollow**, an underground street under the ice. Walk it in first person; when a
name plate shows your key, press **E** to use that place. What you earned on the ice (Rime Essence and
banked Heartstone) is spent here.

## Shops

<div class="wrap-table">

| Shop | Sells |
|---|---|
| [Outfitter](/wiki/hollow/outfitter/) | Helmets, boots, gloves and backpacks |
| [Pick Smith](/wiki/hollow/pick-smith/) | Pickaxe upgrades |
| [Tower Works](/wiki/hollow/tower-works/) | Tower upgrades |
| [Quartermaster](/wiki/hollow/quartermaster/) | Supplies for your next match |

</div>

## Stations

<div class="wrap-table">

| Station | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Sealed Gate** | The Company's lower gate |
| **Signal Relay** | Reach the other Hollows (host or join) ([online guide](/wiki/playing-online/)) |
| **Surface Lift** | Ride the cage up to the ice (solo) |
| **Library** | Read the Almanac and your relic collection ([relics](/wiki/relics/)) |
| **Trainer** | Spend skill points with your mentor ([skill tree](/wiki/skills/)) |
| **Records** | Your achievements ([list](/wiki/achievements/)) |
| **Stats Board** | Your lifetime stats |
| **Survey Table** | The Company's map and your next goals |

</div>

Rime Essence: the pale wisps enemies drop. Walk over them before they fade. Heartstone: every boss drops one, and about 1 in 30 gray rocks. Carry it to a Base screen and press E to bank it.
