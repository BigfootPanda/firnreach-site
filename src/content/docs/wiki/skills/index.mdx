Your **Trainer** in the Hollow keeps the skill tree. You earn **skill points** by leveling up (two a level past
level 100) and spend them on skills. Respec is free: every point comes back.

- The tree grows out from your level: each branch opens at a level, and deeper skills can need a higher level still.
- Skills you can't reach yet are hidden in fog until your level and their prerequisite are met.
- At level 100 the **master classes** open, one for each branch plus the Hunter.
- The level cap is 100. **Prestige** at the cap starts you over at level 1 with your skills cleared and raises
  the cap by 5 (up to 20 times). You keep relics, achievements, lifetime stats and your name.

The whole tree costs **377 points**.
