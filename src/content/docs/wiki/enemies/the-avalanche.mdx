## How to spot its attack

An orange ring fills in on the ground for 1.5 s. When it turns icy blue, guns inside fire much slower and reach shorter for 5 s. Pick a gun up or move it out.

> A mountain's worth of snow and rock that learned to walk toward the song: the first colossus. When it roars, the storm comes with it. You get a breath of warning. Use it.

## Ability

Blizzard: every 15 s, after a warning ring, guns within ~7 cells take 60% longer between shots and reach 25% shorter for 5 s. Carried guns are never affected.
