Skarnholt left things in the ice. Walk into one to bring it home: each survivor earns their own, once per profile. Relics restore the Hollow and tell you what happened here. They never change your stats.

In game, a relic you haven't found shows as **? ? ?** with only its hint. The hints are below; open a spoiler
to read the rest. Relics only turn up when the host leaves **Secrets on the ice** on (it is on by default).

## Relic 1

**Hint:** A warm light glints in the rock maze, a short walk from where the cage sets you down. Walk into it to bring it home.

<details>
<summary>Spoiler: what it is</summary>

**Frozen Lantern**

A miner's lantern, still faintly lit after who knows how long in the ice. Cut into the glass: three peaks over a closed eye. Skarnholt's mark, the old Company's. They lived under this ice before us, and they closed the eye on purpose.

**In the Hollow:** The Hollow's library alcove lights up and the lantern takes its place on the plinth.

**In the library:** *The Skarnholt Ledger*. Tallies in a tidy hand: red, purple, amber, obsidian, more loads every season. The last line is underlined twice: "Deep shaft opened. Bonus to all crews."

</details>

## Relic 2

**Hint:** After the first night, a survey stake glows far out past the landing site. The log lies beside it.

<details>
<summary>Spoiler: what it is</summary>

**Surveyor's Log**

Water-stained pages in the Company surveyor's hand. The last entry: "The deep light sings at night. The colossi walk toward the song, not toward us. We are the ones making it louder. We sealed the lower gate."

**In the Hollow:** The survey table is restored and the map of the old tunnels goes up on the Hollow's wall.

**In the library:** *Survey Notes, year three*. A careful sketch of a colossus mid-stride, its head turned away from the camp. Under it, in the surveyor's hand: "toward the song."

</details>

## Relic 3

**Hint:** Three echo stones hum somewhere in the maze. Wake all three in one match and follow the sound.

<details>
<summary>Spoiler: what it is</summary>

**Heart of the Hollow**

A shard of blue ice, warm in the hand, humming the note the deep light sings. The echo stones were keeping it. It fits the socket in the Company's gate.

**In the Hollow:** The sealed gate beneath the Hollow opens.

**In the library:** *On Sealing a Gate*. The Company's instructions, half washed out: "...white light, bled cold, poured in the socket... the Heart holds it... do not drill below the second bench."

</details>
